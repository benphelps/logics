# Game Wiki

This wiki explains Logics as it exists in the current build: what each main tab is for, what the player can do there, and which sim rules drive the UI.

## Main Tabs

### My Fleet

Manage the player's trading ships. This is the main hands-on play screen: buy cargo, sell cargo, refuel, repair, hire crew, install upgrades, accept contracts, and send ships between stations. Ships can stay fully manual or switch to automation once they have the right crew.

Read: [`wiki/MY_FLEET.md`](wiki/MY_FLEET.md)

Related: [`wiki/UPGRADES_AND_CREW.md`](wiki/UPGRADES_AND_CREW.md)

### Exchange

A market desk layered over the logistics economy. The player can trade station shares, syndicate shares, commodity spot listings, station basis pairs, futures contracts, and indices with long and short positions, limits, stops, take-profits, dividends, margin, and a trade ledger.

Read: [`wiki/EXCHANGE.md`](wiki/EXCHANGE.md)

### Markets

A commodity logistics view. It shows the goods economy across stations: stock, prices, categories, pressure, and where supply or demand is forming. This is the broad market-reading tab for deciding what should move next.

### Atlas

The map view for stations, ships, and routes. It shows where stations sit, where ships are docked or in transit, and how the network connects. Future route content is expected to make lanes more meaningful, including danger level and combat-relevant locations.

## Core Systems

### Upgrades & Crew

Detailed tables for ship modules, crew roles, crew tiers, offer generation, and the shared modifier system that drives ship stats, automation, maintenance, trading perks, and exchange settlement behavior.

## How To Update This Wiki

When mechanics change, update the page for the tab where the player sees that mechanic first. If a rule is shared by many tabs, document it once on the most relevant page and link to it from the others.
