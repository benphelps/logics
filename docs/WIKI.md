# Game Wiki

This wiki explains Ledgway as it exists in the current build: what each main tab is for, what the player can do there, and which sim rules drive the UI.

## Main Tabs

### Cargo

Cargo is the hands-on ship operations screen. It is where the selected ship buys and sells cargo, refuels, repairs, accepts contracts, installs upgrades, hires crew, toggles auto-pilot, and travels between stations. Shipyards also surface here when the ship is docked at a shipyard station.

Read: [`wiki/MY_FLEET.md`](wiki/MY_FLEET.md)

Related: [`wiki/UPGRADES_AND_CREW.md`](wiki/UPGRADES_AND_CREW.md)

### Exchange

Exchange is the market desk layered over the logistics economy. The player can trade station shares, syndicate shares, commodity spot listings, station basis pairs, futures contracts, and indices with long and short positions, limits, stops, take-profits, dividends, margin, settlement jobs, and navigator trade insights.

Read: [`wiki/EXCHANGE.md`](wiki/EXCHANGE.md)

### Markets

Markets is the commodity logistics screen. It shows universe spot prices, stock depth, low asks, high bids, spread, spot history, producers, consumers, and where supply or demand is forming.

Read: [`wiki/MARKETS.md`](wiki/MARKETS.md)

### Atlas

Atlas is the map and station intelligence screen. It shows stations, routes, ship positions, syndicate territory, lane danger, active events, station sheets, local exchange context, production flow, crew offers, upgrade stock, and shipyard inventory.

Read: [`wiki/ATLAS.md`](wiki/ATLAS.md)

### Ledger

Ledger is the career and history screen. It contains the Charters subtab for manual-action unlocks, Syndicates for faction standing, Combat for encounter history, Log for fleet actions and exchange trades, and the Captain's Ledger sidebar.

Read: [`wiki/LEDGER.md`](wiki/LEDGER.md)

## Core Systems

### Combat

Combat is the hostile-contact system for ships in transit. Manual player ships pause on an encounter modal with fight, flee, and negotiate choices; auto ships resolve by policy. Resolved encounters feed the Ledger Combat tab and Atlas lane danger.

Read: [`wiki/COMBAT.md`](wiki/COMBAT.md)

### Upgrades & Crew

Detailed tables for ship modules, crew roles, crew tiers, milestone gates, offer generation, shipyard hull traits, and the shared modifier system that drives ship stats, automation, maintenance, trading perks, exchange settlement behavior, and combat survivability.

Read: [`wiki/UPGRADES_AND_CREW.md`](wiki/UPGRADES_AND_CREW.md)

## How To Update This Wiki

When mechanics change, update the page for the tab where the player sees that mechanic first. If a rule is shared by many tabs, document it once on the most relevant page and link to it from the others.
